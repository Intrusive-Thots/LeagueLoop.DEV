## 2026-05-31 - [Reflex Trainer Mini-Game in Matchmaking]
**Learning:** Gamifying inactive user states (like waiting in a matchmaking queue) with a contextual mini-game (reaction speed pop-test) keeps users engaged in the app, lowers perceived wait times, and builds anticipation for the actual game queue pop.
**Action:** Identify points in client-server lifecycles where users are waiting passively, and introduce lightweight reflex or prediction elements to sustain engagement.
